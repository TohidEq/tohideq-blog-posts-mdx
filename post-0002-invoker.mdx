## What is Baby Invoker?

**Baby Invoker** (officially known as the **Acolyte of the Lost Arts**) is a cosmetic **Persona** item for the hero Invoker in Valve's game *Dota 2*.

It was the first Persona ever released in the game, debuting during The International 2019 Battle Pass season[citation:11][citation:15]. The item transforms the adult Invoker model into his younger, adolescent self—a prodigy at an ancient wizard's academy—complete with a new voice, custom animations, and a redesigned Forged Spirit[citation:15].

## Key Features

- **New Model**: Replaces Invoker's default appearance with a younger version of the hero.
- **Custom Assets**: Includes unique hero portraits, ability icons, a minimap icon, and attack particles[citation:15].
- **Animations**: All-new animations tailored to the younger model[citation:15].
- **Forged Spirit**: The summoned spirit is also visually updated to match the Persona theme[citation:15].

## How Was It Obtained?

The Baby Invoker was originally available as a reward for reaching **Battle Pass Level 305** during the 2019 Battle Pass season[citation:15]. It was not sold as a standalone store item.

Because it was tied to a limited-time event, it cannot be purchased directly from the Steam Community Market. Players who still have the item can only transfer it through specific gift functions or by trading accounts (though account trading is against Valve's Terms of Service).

## Technical Notes

The Persona uses a distinct model and animation set. Some players have noted subtle differences in the "feel" of the hero's attack projectile speed and animation compared to the default adult Invoker, which is a known quirk of the Persona system[citation:2].
